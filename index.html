Artisan Heritage & Technique

The alchemy of teak, mud, and desert indigo.

In the ancient printing townships outside Jaipur, craft is an uninterrupted continuum. A single bedsheet passes through more than five artisan households—from master block carvers who chisel teak wood to millimeter precision, to the printers who apply steady downward force on heavy wooden tables.

01
Teak Block Relief
Aged seasoned teak carved by hand with fine gouges to create relief patterns.
02
Dabu Mud Resist
Black clay, lime, and gum arabic mixed to resist natural dye immersion.
03
Rhythmic Stamping
Aligned by eye with breath-like precision across seven meters of cotton.
04
Sun Curing
Dried under the Rajasthan sun to naturally oxidize mineral pigments.
